Delete your QuickServe account

This page explains how to delete your QuickServe customer account and what happens to your data.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the QuickServe app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password, and confirm.

Can't use the app?

Email balakrishnan.srini64@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my QuickServe account". We will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • All of your bookings. Upcoming appointments are cancelled and their time slots are released.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We keep no copy of your personal data after deletion. See the privacy policy for details.
