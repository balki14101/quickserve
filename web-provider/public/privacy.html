QuickServe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

QuickServe is a booking app that lets customers book appointments with local service providers such as salons, repair shops, tutors, and fitness trainers. This policy explains what information the QuickServe customer app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide when you sign up: your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your password, stored only in hashed form. We cannot read it.
  • Your booking activity: the services, time slots, and status of the appointments you make.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment details.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To make, show, and cancel your bookings.
  • To let the provider you book with see your name, email, and phone number so they can serve you.

Sharing

Your name, email, and phone number are visible to the providers you book with. We do not sell your information, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in a MongoDB Atlas database and served by an API hosted on Render. A sign-in token is kept in your device's secure storage so you stay logged in, and is removed when you log out.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time from the app (Account, then Delete account) or by following the steps on the account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your profile and your bookings.

Children

QuickServe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
